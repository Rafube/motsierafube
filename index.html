Chapter 5

Data model

A star schema: one fact table of sales transactions surrounded by four dimensions. Each dimension relates to the fact table one-to-many. The grain is one record per sales transaction.

Keys: PK primary key, FK foreign key. 1 marks the dimension side of a relationship, * the fact side.

Calculated measures (DAX)

Total Revenue

Revenue in the selected period.

SUM(Fact_Sales[Revenue])

Total Cost

Cost in the selected period.

SUM(Fact_Sales[Cost])

Total Profit

Revenue minus cost.

[Total Revenue] - [Total Cost]

Profit Margin %

Profit as a share of revenue. DIVIDE guards against zero.

DIVIDE([Total Profit],[Total Revenue],0)

Total Units Sold

Quantity sold in the period.

SUM(Fact_Sales[Units Sold])

Data quality rules

DQ001Revenue cannot be negative
DQ002Cost cannot be negative
DQ003BranchID must exist in Dim_Branch
DQ004ProductID must exist in Dim_Product
DQ005SalespersonID must exist in Dim_Salesperson
DQ006Transaction dates cannot be blank
DQ007Units Sold must be zero or more
DQ008Profit must reconcile with revenue and cost
